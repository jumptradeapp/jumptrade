Beta terms and privacy

Plain-English version. Last updated November 2026.

What this is

jumptrade is a private beta. You were invited personally. Things will break, change or disappear while it's being built, and your feedback shapes what it becomes.

Not investment advice

  • Everything in the app is information and ideas, not advice. Scores, setups, buy, stop and target levels are produced by rules, not by someone who knows your situation.
  • Prices and data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Check with your broker before you trade.
  • Trading involves real risk, including losing more than you expect. You're responsible for your own decisions.

Please don't share it

Your invite and sign-in codes are for you. Don't forward the link, post screenshots publicly or give access to anyone else. Access can be turned off at any time.

What we store

  • Your first name, so the app can greet you and the owner knows who's testing.
  • Your device type (for example "iPhone, Safari") and when you use the app, to understand how it's used and fix problems.
  • Your watchlist and alert settings, so alerts can watch your stocks.
  • Feedback you send, and error reports if something breaks in the app.

We don't ask for your email, phone number, brokerage login, account balances or payment details, and we don't sell or share your information. The app doesn't use advertising trackers.

Leaving

Sign out from the menu at any time. Ask the person who invited you and your account and data will be deleted.

BETA TESTING · NOT FOR PUBLIC USE. Do not share with unauthorized users.
